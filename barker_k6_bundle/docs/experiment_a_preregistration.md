# Pre-registration — Experiment (a): the depth-attenuation test of Q6.2.B

*Locked before the first enumeration CPU-hour. Amends and supersedes any
informal criterion stated earlier. The point of this document is that the
decision rule exists in writing before the empirical data does.*

## Question

Is the +6.6 pp transversal over-selection at (3,3,3,3) — and its apparent
attenuation as cofactor depth rises — a combinatorial consequence of
{covering, minimal, zero-δ} conditioning on the reciprocity-symmetric skeleton,
or does a depth-graded **arithmetic** signal survive that the skeleton model
provably cannot produce?

## The observable and the decision cell

For a target x in a k=5 zero-δ minimal covering S, with t_x = 3, all-QNR
cofactor (all four χ_x-values odd), and w_x = 2:

    R(profile) = P(σ_x = 0 | the above, cofactor depth profile = `profile`)

**Decision cell: (3,3,3,4) alone.** The (3,3,3,5) cell is declared
**non-decisive** in advance: even at the reachable N it cannot separate 0.253
from 0.200, and its point estimate would tempt over-reading. It will be reported
but not interpreted.

## The benchmark is N-free by construction (set-locality lemma)

The decision compares the empirical (3,3,3,4) rate, across N, to a **single
constant** model benchmark. That constant carries no N-dependence — and this is a
lemma, not an assumption. The conditioning is profile-internal (depths fixed to
(3,3,3,3,4)), and every predicate defining R — σ_x, all-QNR, w_x=2, zero-δ,
covering, and the minimality check over all proper subsets — is S-internal:
covering- and minimality-witnesses lie in S, and no predicate references a prime
outside S. Hence R is a function of the value-matrix distribution given the
profile alone, which in the skeleton is {reciprocity, uniform higher digits} with
no N. This contrasts with the E2 census, which summed over
`V_x = {p in the whole universe : χ_x(p)=0}` — a universe-wide object that
genuinely drifts with N. A toy-N global sampler would add noise, not assurance,
and is skipped; the empirical-trajectory-vs-constant comparison is rigorous.

## Empirical design: trajectory, not a single point

Enumerate zero-δ k=5 minimal coverings and extract the (3,3,3,4) cell at
**N ∈ {160, 200, 240, 260}** (N=160 already in cache: 49/218 = 0.225). The
decisive evidence is the **trajectory**: does empirical R(3,3,3,4) converge to
the N-invariant R_model as N grows (dissolution), or plateau below it
(arithmetic)? This is the E2 ℓ=3-ratio trajectory (0.62→0.98) applied to this
cell.

## Power

The decision compares empirical (3,3,3,4) to the **locked model 0.266**. The
existing N=160 empirical (49/218 = 0.225) sits Δ = −0.041, **−1.43σ** below it
(combined σ = 0.029) — a non-significant hint of real attenuation. Resolving an
effect of this size at 3σ requires combined σ < 0.0137 → empirical **n ≈ 1000**,
reached at **N ≈ 280–300** at the observed growth rate. (The earlier n≈600 plan
would have died at an ambiguous ~2.4σ.) The (3,3,3,4) cell count is the gating
quantity; report it, and the trajectory across N, at each N.

## Decision rule (three pre-registered branches, fixed now)

Let Δ = R_emp(3,3,3,4) − R_model(3,3,3,4) at the decision N, with combined σ.

1. **Consistent** (|Δ| ≤ 2σ, trajectory flat-to-converging): the attenuation was
   over-read on n=218/38; **dissolution complete** — Q6.2.B is combinatorial at
   every observable and scale tested.
2. **Significantly below** (Δ < −3σ, trajectory plateaus below R_model): a
   **located, depth-graded arithmetic signal** the skeleton provably cannot
   produce, against a validated combinatorial null that fails — the strong claim,
   and sharper than the current attenuation paragraph because it ships with the
   null that breaks.
3. **Significantly above** (Δ > +3σ): **model defect or positive depth effect —
   halt and diagnose before interpreting.** No narrative until the cause is found.

## Stopping rule (amendment after Error #4 — jointly held)

The original design fixed the branches and the power target but not a stopping
rule, and a dissolution was declared at interim power (N=240, n=1546, −0.39σ),
landed in the manuscript thesis, and retracted when the registered trajectory
completed at N=280 (cumulative −1.9σ; largest disjoint sample −2.4σ). The fix:

- **No branch may be declared while any registered point is still enumerating.**
- **The decision n must be met on disjoint post-registration data, not on
  cumulative data that includes the sample which motivated the test.** Under this
  clause even the N=240 call was illegitimate: n=1546 included the N=160 cell
  that flagged the question.

The power target was necessary, not sufficient; the registered trajectory was the
design.

## Result of record (trajectory through N=280)

Disjoint marginal increments vs the flat benchmark 0.266 ± 0.004:
49/218 = 0.225 (−1.4σ), 125/428 = 0.292 (+1.2σ), 230/900 = 0.256 (−0.7σ),
440/1833 = 0.240 (−2.4σ). Four samples homogeneous (χ² = 5.9, 3 df) near 0.250;

## Power, both sides (amendment)

A 3σ decision on a −1.6 pp effect is constrained from both sides: at the current
model depth (σ = 0.0041), infinite empirical data caps the achievable z near
3.9σ. The registered design needs **model n ≈ 45,000 (σ → 0.002)** alongside
**empirical n ≈ 16,000 disjoint** (≈ N = 380). Cheap on the model side (sampler,
not enumerator).

## Step 2′ — within-row family-profile diagnostic (registered before measuring)

All-QNR multiset taxonomy at t = 3 (values in {1,3,5,7}, negation classes {1,7},
{3,5}), graded by within-row value-coincidence:
- w=0: {a,a,a,a} (4), {a,a,a,b} (3,1), {a,a,b,b} (2,2)  [b ≠ ā]
- w=1: {c,c,a,ā} (2,1,1)
- w=2: transversal {1,3,5,7} (1,1,1,1, σ=0) vs {a,a,ā,b} (2,1,1, σ≠0)
- w=3: {a,a,a,ā} (3,1)
- w=4: {a,a,ā,ā} (2,2)

These are **eight shape-types, not eight orbits.** Three of the eight each split
into two distinct orbits under the unit action, so the orbit count is **eleven**
(see the unit-symmetry lemma below):

| shape-type | orbits | representatives |
|---|---|---|
| w=0 {a,a,a,a} | 1 | (1,1,1,1) |
| w=0 {a,a,a,b} | **2** | (1,1,1,3), (1,1,1,5) |
| w=0 {a,a,b,b} | **2** | (1,1,3,3), (1,1,5,5) |
| w=1 {c,c,a,ā} | 1 | (1,1,3,5) |
| w=2 transversal | 1 | (1,3,5,7) |
| w=2 {a,a,ā,b} | **2** | (1,1,3,7), (1,1,5,7) |
| w=3 {a,a,a,ā} | 1 | (1,1,1,7) |
| w=4 {a,a,ā,ā} | 1 | (1,1,7,7) |

Counting shape-types as orbits is what produced the earlier eight-orbit claim.
Wherever this document says "per orbit" it means the eleven, not the eight.

The deficit (shortfall of the all-distinct transversal in w=2) is, in this
language, an **excess of within-row value-coincidence**. Procedure, fixed now:
1. Measure the partition-type profile per w-cell, **empirical vs model under
   identical conditioning** (the null is the model's own conditional rows, not
   iid-uniform).
2. If coincidence-excess appears in the unspent w=0/w=1 cells, fit a single
   coincidence parameter ρ on **w=0/w=1 alone**, then **register ρ's out-of-sample
   prediction for the w=2 transversal fraction before comparing it to 0.250.**
3. Outcomes: one ρ predicting 0.250 from disjoint cells ⇒ a one-parameter
   within-column coincidence mechanism unifying the deficit across all cells (the
   question becomes "what arithmetic produces column coincidence-excess," better
   posed for the N≈380 decision). w=0/w=1 matching the model with the deficit
   confined to w=2 ⇒ a narrow signature pointing at benchmark calibration (the
   QNR-composition post-stratification returns as prime suspect), and N≈380 likely
   buys nothing.

## Amendment (2026-06-11) — cluster-robust σ, fourth branch, channel bound, unit-symmetry

**Cluster error (corrects all prior σ).** Every cell count is a count of *targets*,
but targets are bundled in *configurations* sharing one value matrix (a pure-t3
5-set gives up to 5 rows to (3,3,3,3); a {3,3,3,3,4} set gives up to 4 to
(3,3,3,4)). All σ quoted this session — including the −1.9σ and the earlier
−0.39σ — treated rows as independent Bernoullis. Henceforth every decision σ is
**cluster-robust, clustered by configuration**, for empirical and model alike.
The cluster audit is run first and rescales the question.

**Fourth branch — BOUNDED-UNDECIDABLE** (the most likely landing zone, which the
original 3-branch rule did not cover). If, after cluster-robust correction and
depth-4 post-stratification, the deficit is 2σ < |Δ| < 3σ (or smaller) and the
channel bound (below) excludes pairwise arithmetic as its source, report a
**bound, not a binary.** Drafted sentence: *"After cluster-robust correction and
depth-4 post-stratification the depth-(3,3,3,4) deficit is __ pp at __σ,
consistent with zero. A channel-bound argument (row values are unconditionally
iid by joint Chebotarev; conditioning can induce row structure only through
pairwise character links, measured at Cramér's V ≈ 0.03 in the even sector and
null in the odd sector where these rows live) shows any deficit larger than ~__ pp
would require multi-point correlations among real characters at distinct moduli —
Chowla-adjacent territory. Arithmetic beyond quadratic reciprocity therefore
contributes at most ~__ pp, in one cell, consistent with zero."*

**Channel bound.** Unconditionally a row χ_x(p₁..p₄) is iid-uniform (Frobenius
classes of distinct primes in one fixed extension; joint Chebotarev — solid, not
heuristic). Row structure can be induced only by conditioning through cross-column
pairwise links: mod-2 reciprocity (in the model) plus measured higher pairwise
laws (V ≈ 0.03, even sector; null, odd sector). Propagate the measured pairwise
deviations through the conditioning at measured size and bound |ΔR|; a deficit
exceeding that envelope requires multi-point character correlations.

**Unit-symmetry lemma (manuscript-bound regardless of outcome).** Every census
predicate (witness v_i+v_j≡0, w, zero-δ, σ=0) is invariant under rescaling a row
by a unit u ∈ (Z/8)*, which acts transitively on {1,3,5,7}.

*What that does and does not give.* Predicate invariance alone does **not** force
the conditioned marginal to be uniform; it says only that the conditioning EVENT
is unit-invariant. Uniformity additionally requires the *unconditional* row law to
be unit-invariant. Under the joint-Chebotarev premise recorded in the channel
bound below — rows unconditionally iid-uniform, so within-QNR iid-uniform on
{1,3,5,7} — the diagonal unit action preserves the unconditional law; conditioning
on an invariant event then leaves the law invariant, and transitivity on {1,3,5,7}
makes the single-value marginal uniform. Note the conclusion is a statement about
the **population** law under that premise, not about the **empirical** distribution
of any finite census: sampling deviation from uniform in the data is expected and
is not evidence against the lemma.

*Orbit count (corrected: eleven, not eight).* The invariant content of the row
distribution up to units is the set of orbits of size-4 multisets from {1,3,5,7}
under the diagonal action. Each non-identity unit acts on {1,3,5,7} as a
fixed-point-free product of two transpositions (u=3: 1↔3, 5↔7; u=5: 1↔5, 3↔7;
u=7: 1↔7, 3↔5), so a multiset is fixed by it exactly when its multiplicities are
constant on that involution's two orbits — 3 fixed multisets per non-identity
unit. Burnside gives

    (35 + 3 + 3 + 3) / 4  =  44 / 4  =  11.

Orbit sizes: seven of size 4, three of size 2 ({1,1,3,3}, {1,1,5,5}, {1,1,7,7} up
to units), one of size 1; 7·4 + 3·2 + 1 = 35. The transversal {1,3,5,7} is the
unique singleton orbit — which is why R is unit-invariant.

R (the transversal fraction) is unit-invariant, so a real deficit must appear as
an **orbit-weight tilt** and nowhere else. This gives §6.2 its canonical
coordinates: the residue question is exactly "are the orbit weights the
skeleton's orbit weights."

**Corrected experimental order:** (1) cluster audit [running]; (2) family
profiles across all eleven orbits, pure-(3,3,3,3) as control — fit coincidence
tilt λ on w ∈ {1,3,4} (the w-cell weights; cubic-exponent families amplify λ to
~28% shifts, powered at current n), out-of-sample predict the w=2 ratio; (3)
depth-4 post-stratification of the existing 11,821 model configs by the depth-4
element's relational profile; (4) only then the (now likely moot) D3-augmented
run, or N≈380 — and only if a cluster-corrected, stratification-surviving deficit
remains.

## Amendment 2 (2026-06-11) — population trap, maximal-pairwise, branch wording

**Population trap (would be Error #5 if walked into).** The χ²=39.4 D3 signal was
measured on *kernel-cohabiting* depth-3 pairs (the A1-cycle population). The
sampler draws *universe* pairs and applies {covering, minimal, zero-δ}
downstream. These are different populations, and H6 (all hard pairs, no
cohabitation) found the even-even sector flat (χ²≈3). If the dependence exists
only in the cohabiting population, it is a **three-point** effect (external hub
kernel conditioning correlating with the pair's internal values) that the model
genuinely lacks but which **cannot be injected as a pair-joint** without
double-counting the conditioning the sampler already does — that would fabricate
a "Yes."

**Gate before any build (data in hand).** Measure the *universe-pair* joints, full
support including 0, at N=320, for every depth-pair upstream of the deficit:
(3,3) and (3,4)/(4,3), both sectors. The deficit concentrates at p4-witness=2,
governed by χ_{p4}(a)+χ_{p4}(b) mod 16 — i.e. the **(3,4) joint** (Z/8 × Z/16),
never examined this session (all prior value-law tests were depth-(3,3)). Flat
everywhere ⇒ no universe pairwise law; skip the build; the deficit is certified
≥3-point or sub-threshold at zero CPU, and the cohabiting χ²=39.4 is reframed as
the three-point measurement it was. Non-trivial anywhere ⇒ proceed.

**Upgrade: maximal-pairwise model.** Inject *all* non-trivial measured universe
joints (per ordered depth-pair, per sector, full support incl 0; sample the
both-nonzero conditional derived from the full joint, not pre-restricted on
{2,4,6}²). If it reproduces R_emp and the p4-witness=2 concentration, the deficit
is pairwise — one ablation pass (re-independentize one joint at a time) names the
component. If it still misses, the residual is **certifiably ≥3-point**, and the
channel bound becomes *empirical*: the measured residual of the best possible
pairwise model.

**Corrected branch wording (the abstract depends on this distinction):**
- **PAIRWISE-CLOSED** (maximal-pairwise reproduces R_emp): the skeleton was
  *incomplete, not miscalibrated*. A measured universe pairwise law beyond
  reciprocity (V≈0.03, unproven) closes the gap — that law **is arithmetic**.
  Thesis: "one proven layer (quadratic reciprocity) plus one measured pairwise
  law, origin open."
- **MISCALIBRATION** (the cohabiting dependence is selection-induced, no universe
  law exists): the skeleton was right; the cohabiting χ²=39.4 is a three-point
  conditioning artifact; the deficit is sub-threshold noise.
- **≥3-POINT / BOUNDED** (universe pairwise non-trivial but maximal-pairwise still
  misses): residual certifiably needs multi-point correlations; report the
  empirical bound.

Predictions register verbatim only **after** the gate names which joints are
non-trivial — they cannot be fixed before the population is known.
